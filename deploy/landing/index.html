How it works

From scan to bill, without anyone writing an order down

  1. Guest scans

    Each table has its own QR. The menu opens in the phone's browser with photos, veg marks and sizes.

  2. Guest orders

    They pick items and add-ons and place the order. They can also call a waiter, ask for water or the bill.

  3. Kitchen cooks

    The order appears on the kitchen screen with a sound. A tap moves it from New to Preparing to Served.

  4. You bill

    Bill one order or the whole table, print it, and get paid by UPI straight into your account.

Questions owners ask

Before you decide

Do my guests need to download an app or sign up?

No. They scan the QR and the menu opens in their phone's browser. They type their name and phone number once, when they place their first order.

Where does the guests' money go?

Straight to your own UPI account. We never hold your money and take no commission on orders. When a guest taps "I've paid", your staff checks the UPI app and marks it received.

My guests are older or prefer ordering from a waiter.

That still works. Your waiter scans the table's QR on their own phone and places the order for the guest. It reaches the kitchen screen and the table's bill like any other order.

Can I change prices or mark an item sold out during service?

Yes, from your phone, any time. Guests see the change immediately; there is nothing to reprint.

Will the kitchen staff see my sales?

No. The kitchen signs in with its own PIN and only sees the orders it has to cook. Changing the PIN signs every kitchen screen out.

What happens after the 14-day trial?

Pick Monthly, Half Yearly or Yearly from "My plan" and pay online. If you don't, guests can still see your menu but can't place orders until you choose a plan. Your menu and data stay safe.

What if I renew late?

A paid plan gets 7 extra days in which guests can still order, so a late renewal never stops service. Renew early and the new period starts after the current one ends, so you lose no days.

Does it work without internet?

No. Ordering, the kitchen screen and billing need an internet connection. Mobile data on the kitchen phone is enough.

I have more than one outlet.

Each outlet has its own plan, menu and QR codes. Talk to us for a multi-outlet price.
