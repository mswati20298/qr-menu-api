Questions

Quick answers

Do guests need an app?

No. They scan and the menu opens in their phone's browser.

Where does the guests' money go?

Straight to your UPI account. We take no commission.

Older guests prefer a waiter.

The waiter can place the order from their own phone. It reaches the kitchen the same way.

Can I change prices during service?

Yes, from your phone. Guests see it at once.

What if I'm not happy?

Ask within 7 days of your first plan and we refund it in full. Refund policy

What happens after the 3-day trial?

Pick a plan in "My plan". Until then guests can see the menu but not order.

Does it work without internet?

No. Mobile data on the kitchen phone is enough.
