Refund and cancellation policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

This policy covers payments made to QRenvo, operated by Mahesh Kumar (Proprietor) for QRenvo plans, setup and printed QR codes.

Free trial

The trial is free. No payment is taken, so there is nothing to cancel or refund.

Cancelling a plan

  • Plans do not renew automatically, so you never need to cancel to avoid the next charge.
  • If you stop using QRenvo, your plan stays active until the end of the period you paid for.

Refunds on plans

  • Within 7 days of your first paid plan: if QRenvo does not work for your restaurant, ask for a refund from My plan in your admin panel (or write to us) and we refund the plan amount less the payment gateway charges. This applies once per restaurant.
  • Payment gateway charges are not refundable. Razorpay charges about 2% + GST on every online payment and does not return it when a payment is refunded. Example: on a ₹499 plan you get about ₹487 back. My plan shows the exact amount before you ask.
  • A refunded plan stops at once: customers can no longer place orders.
  • After 7 days, unused months are not refunded.
  • Charged twice or in error: always refunded in full, including the gateway charges.
  • Paid by UPI, bank transfer or cash directly to us (not through Razorpay): no gateway charges apply, so the full plan amount is refunded.

How the refund amount is worked out

We do not keep any part of your refund. The only deduction is the charge Razorpay (our payment gateway) takes on the original payment, which Razorpay does not give back to us when we refund. We read the exact charge for your payment from Razorpay; if it cannot be read, we use 2% + 18% GST.

The exact figures for your payment are shown on My plan before you send a request, so there are no surprises.

What happens after you ask

  1. You send a request from My plan with a short reason. You see the amount you will get back.
  2. We review it, usually within 1 working day, and approve or reject it. If we reject it, the reason is shown on My plan.
  3. Once approved, the plan stops at once and Razorpay sends the money to the same card, UPI or bank account you paid from.
  4. The money reaches your account within 5 to 7 working days (this depends on your bank). My plan shows the status: waiting for review, on its way, or refunded.

Setup fee and printed QR codes

  • The setup fee is not refundable once setup work (menu loading, training) has started.
  • Printed QR stickers, tent cards or standees that arrive damaged or with a printing mistake are replaced free if you tell us within 3 days of delivery. Printed items are made for your restaurant and are not refundable otherwise.

Guest orders

Payments guests make for food go directly to the restaurant's UPI account. QRenvo does not receive this money, so refunds for food orders are handled by the restaurant.

How to ask for a refund

The quickest way: log in, open My plan and press Request refund next to the payment.

You can also email mswati20298@gmail.com or WhatsApp +91 96702 26608 with your restaurant name, the email on your account and the payment date or Razorpay payment ID. Approved refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 to 7 working days.
